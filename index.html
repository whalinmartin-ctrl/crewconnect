About Apex Protocol

Professional service with a human touch.

We believe protocol should never feel cold or complicated. It should make people feel welcomed, respected and confidently guided. From the first arrival to the final departure, our team brings structure, warmth and composure to every assignment.

Founder & CEO

Leadership

Blaise Ishimwe

Blaise leads Apex Protocol with a simple idea: when people trust you with an important moment, your job is to make them feel supported — not surrounded by logistics. His focus is on preparation, discretion and a team culture that stays calm when the room gets busy.
